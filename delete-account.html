Delete your Money Monitor account

You can request deletion of your Money Monitor account and all associated data at any time. This page is provided by EFilingexpert, the developer of Money Monitor.

How to request deletion

Send an email to office.aqbohra@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my Money Monitor account" from the email address you registered with.

We will verify the request and delete your account within 7 working days. You will receive a confirmation email once it is complete.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email, sign-in credentials)
  • All expenses, categories, notes and analytics you recorded
  • Your company / approver settings (if you created a business account)
  • Any receipt images you saved

What may be retained

Anonymised, non-identifiable merchant-category learnings (for example, that a merchant name maps to a spending category) are retained because they cannot be linked back to you. No personal data is kept after deletion, except where we are legally required to retain records.

Alternative: delete data only

If you want to keep your account but erase your expense history, email the same address with the subject "Clear my Money Monitor data".
