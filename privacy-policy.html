Privacy Policy — Money Monitor

Last updated: 28 May 2026

Money Monitor (the "App") is operated by EFilingexpert ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what personal information the App collects, why, how we protect it, and your rights. By using the App you agree to this policy. This policy is governed by the laws of India, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, account type (personal/business), and (for business accounts) company name. Collected when you register or sign in (including via Google Sign-In).
  • Financial / transaction data — expense amounts, merchant/supplier names, categories, GST amounts, dates, and notes that you enter or import.
  • Receipt images — photos you capture or select for receipt scanning (OCR). Text is extracted on-device; you choose whether to save the result.
  • SMS data (Android only, optional) — if you grant SMS permission, the App reads bank/transaction SMS solely on your device to help you auto-fill an expense. We do not transmit, upload, or store the full contents of your SMS messages on our servers. You can use the App fully without granting this permission (manual and photo entry).
  • Authentication tokens — managed securely by our authentication provider to keep you signed in.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and (for business plans) your organization and approvers.
  • To store, display, and analyze the expenses you record.
  • To provide features such as analytics, approvals, GST summaries, and report export.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the App.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Where your data is stored

Your account and transaction data are stored using Supabase (a hosted PostgreSQL platform) on servers located in the Asia-Pacific (Mumbai, India) region. Access is protected by row-level security so each user can access only their own data (and, for business accounts, data their role permits).

4. Sharing and disclosure

We share data only with service providers that operate the App on our behalf (authentication, database hosting, and — if you sign in with Google — Google for sign-in only), and only as needed to provide the service. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may delete your account at any time, by emailing us at the address below. On account deletion we remove your personal data from our active systems.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted transport (HTTPS), authenticated access, and database row-level security. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your data, and you may withdraw consent (for example, by revoking SMS or camera permission in your device settings). To exercise these rights, contact us below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
